About PixSathi

India's free exam photo & signature resizer — simple, fast, and 100% private.

PixSathi is a free online exam photo & signature resizer built for Indian exam students. Our goal is to give you all the photo and signature tools you need to fill exam forms — without any signup, without any payment, and without any privacy risk.

Whether you are filling out a form for SSC, IBPS, GATE, NEET, JEE, UPSC, or any other exam — everything you need is available on PixSathi.

Our Mission

In India, resizing photos and signatures for exam forms is very difficult. Most websites are:

  • Slow
  • Full of ads
  • Ask for signup
  • Upload files to their server
  • Don't give the exact size

PixSathi is the solution to all these problems. Our mission is simple:

  • 100% Free: No signup, no payment, no hidden charges
  • Privacy First: Files are processed on your device — never on a server
  • Fast: No waiting — instant results
  • Exam-Ready: Exact size for every exam
  • Made for India: Built for the needs of Indian exam students
