Why Do Photos Get Rejected?

Every year, thousands of candidates face rejection due to simple mistakes. Here are the most common reasons:

  • Wrong File Size: If your photo is larger than 50 KB or smaller than 20 KB, it will not be accepted.
  • Incorrect Dimensions: The system requires specific pixel dimensions. A photo that is too wide or too tall will fail.
  • Blurry or Low-Quality Image: The photo must be clear, with a plain background and no shadows.
  • Signature Issues: The signature must be in black ink on white paper. It should not be too small or too large.
  • Wrong Format: Only JPEG or JPG formats are accepted. PNG or PDF files will be rejected.

Tips for Taking the Perfect Photo

  • Use a plain white or light-colored background.
  • Ensure your face is clearly visible, with no shadows.
  • Do not wear a cap or sunglasses.
  • Use a recent photo (not older than 3 months).
  • For the signature, sign on a white paper with a black pen and scan it at high resolution.
