UPSC Photo Size 2026: Requirements & Resizer

Published on: 02 Nov 2026 | Category: Tutorials

Applying for the UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) is the first step toward a prestigious career in the Indian administrative services. However, the application process is highly detailed, and even a minor mistake in uploading your photograph or signature can lead to the rejection of your form. The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has strict guidelines regarding the dimensions, file size, and format of the uploaded documents. In this guide, we will provide you with the exact UPSC photo size 2026 requirements and a step-by-step method to ensure your images are accepted without any issues.

Whether you are a first-time aspirant or appearing for the exam again, getting the documents right is crucial for a smooth application process.

Detailed UPSC Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: The photo should ideally be around 350 x 450 pixels.
  • File Size: The image must be between 20 KB and 300 KB. A file that is too small or too large will be rejected.
  • Format: Only JPEG or JPG formats are accepted. PNG or PDF files will not work.
  • Background: Use a plain white or light-colored background.
  • Face: Your face should be clearly visible and facing the camera. Avoid wearing sunglasses or caps.
  • Recency: The photo should be recent, preferably taken within the last 3 months.
  • Quality: The photo should be of high quality and not blurry.

Detailed UPSC Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: The signature should be around 350 x 350 pixels.
  • File Size: The image must be between 20 KB and 300 KB.
  • Ink: Use black ink on white paper.
  • Style: The signature should be your running handwriting, not in capital letters.
  • Clarity: The signature must be clear and readable.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong File Size: A file size outside the 20-300 KB range will be rejected.
  • Blurry Image: The photo and signature must be clear. Avoid using a low-quality camera.
  • Old Photo: Use a recent photo, not one that is several years old.
  • Capital Letters in Signature: Always use your natural running handwriting.
  • Wrong Format: Only JPEG or JPG is accepted. Do not use PNG or PDF.
