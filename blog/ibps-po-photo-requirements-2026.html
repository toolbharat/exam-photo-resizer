IBPS PO Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: 200 width × 230 height pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 50 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Background: Plain white ya light
  • Face: Camera ki taraf seedha
  • Recent: 3 mahine ke andar
  • No glasses/cap: Bilkul nahi

IBPS PO Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: 140 × 60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Ink: Black ink, white paper par
  • Style: Running handwriting (CAPITAL nahi)
  • Clear: Saaf aur readable

IBPS PO Thumb Impression Requirements

  • Dimensions: 240 × 240 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 50 KB
  • Finger: Left thumb
  • Ink: Black ya blue
  • Clear: Saaf print, blur nahi
