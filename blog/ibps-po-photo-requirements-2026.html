Filling out the IBPS PO form? Photo, signature, and thumb impression — all three need the exact size. If even one is wrong, the form gets rejected.

In this guide: IBPS PO photo size 2026, signature size, thumb impression size, step-by-step resize guide, and common mistakes — everything.

⚡ Quick Answer: IBPS PO photo = 200×230 pixels (20-50 KB), signature = 140×60 pixels (10-20 KB), thumb impression = 240×240 pixels (20-50 KB).

IBPS PO Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: 200 width × 230 height pixels
  • File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Background: Plain white or light
  • Face: Looking straight at the camera
  • Recent: Taken within 3 months
  • No glasses/cap: Not at all

IBPS PO Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: 140 × 60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink: Black ink on white paper
  • Style: Running handwriting (not CAPITAL)
  • Clear: Clear and readable

IBPS PO Thumb Impression Requirements

  • Dimensions: 240 × 240 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Finger: Left thumb
  • Ink: Black or blue
  • Clear: Clear print, not blurry

Common Mistakes — IBPS PO

  • Photo other than 100×120 px or 200×230 px — rejected

  • PNG format — rejected
  • CAPITAL letters in signature — rejected
  • Blurry thumb impression — rejected
  • Glasses or cap in photo — rejected
