NEET Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: 200 × 230 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB se 200 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Background: White ya light
  • Face: Camera ki taraf
  • Recent: 3 mahine ke andar
  • Postcard size: 4" × 6" bhi chahiye (exam centre ke liye)

NEET Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: 140 × 60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB se 20 KB
  • Ink: Black, white paper par
  • Style: Running handwriting

NEET Thumb Impression Requirements

  • Dimensions: 240 × 240 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB se 50 KB
  • Finger: Left thumb
  • Ink: Black ya blue
