Filling out the NEET form? Photo, signature, and thumb impression — all three need the exact size and format. Even a small mistake can cause form rejection.

In this guide: NEET photo size 2026, signature size, thumb impression, and a step-by-step guide.

⚡ Quick Answer: NEET photo = 200×230 px (10-200 KB), signature = 140×60 px (10-20 KB), thumb impression = 240×240 px (20-50 KB).

NEET Photo Requirements

  • Dimensions: 200 × 230 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB to 200 KB
  • Format: JPG / JPEG
  • Background: White or light
  • Face: Facing the camera
  • Recent: Taken within 3 months
  • Postcard size: 4" × 6" also required (for exam centre)

NEET Signature Requirements

  • Dimensions: 140 × 60 pixels
  • File size: 10 KB to 20 KB
  • Ink: Black, on white paper
  • Style: Running handwriting

NEET Thumb Impression Requirements

  • Dimensions: 240 × 240 pixels
  • File size: 20 KB to 50 KB
  • Finger: Left thumb
  • Ink: Black or blue
