Exam Photo Rejection: 10 Common Reasons (2026)

← Back to Blog

Exam form bharte waqt photo reject ho gayi? Ye problem har saal lakhon students ke saath hoti hai. Aur karan hamesha common hote hain.

Is guide mein: 10 common reasons jo exam photo rejection ka karan bante hain — aur unse kaise bachein.

Top 10 Exam Photo Rejection Reasons

1. Galat Dimensions

SSC CGL ke liye 100×120 px chahiye, lekin 200×230 px upload karna — direct reject.

2. File Size Zyada

50 KB limit hai, lekin 200 KB ki photo upload karna — reject.

3. Galat Format

SSC sirf JPG/JPEG accept karta hai. PNG, WebP, ya GIF upload karna — reject.

4. Photo Blur / Dhundhli

Low resolution ya blur photo — reject. Face saaf dikhna chahiye.

5. Chashma / Cap

Photo mein chashma ya cap pehna hua — reject. Face clear dikhna chahiye.

6. Purani Photo

6 mahine ya 1 saal purani photo — reject. 3 mahine ke andar ki photo chahiye.

7. Colored Background

Plain white ya light background chahiye. Red, blue, ya dark background — reject.

8. Side Face / Mudda Hua

Camera ki taraf seedha nahi dekhna — reject. Face frontal hona chahiye.

9. Signature mein CAPITAL Letters

Running handwriting chahiye. CAPITAL LETTERS wala signature — reject.

10. Signature Blur

Dhundhla ya halka signature — reject. Black ink, clear handwriting.

⚠️ Important: Exam form fill karne se pehle saari requirements zaroor check karo. Ek chhoti galti form rejection ka karan ban sakti hai.
