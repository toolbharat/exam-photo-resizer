Exam Photo Rejection: 10 Common Reasons (2026)

Published on: 02 Nov 2026 | Category: Exam Tips

Did your photo get rejected while filling out an exam form? This problem happens to millions of students every year. And the reasons are always common. Whether you are applying for SSC, UPSC, NEET, IBPS, or any other competitive exam, the photo and signature requirements are strict. Even a minor mistake can cause your application to be rejected.

In this guide, we will walk you through the 10 most common reasons for exam photo rejection and show you exactly how to avoid them. By the end of this article, you will know how to upload the perfect photo and signature every time.

Top 10 Exam Photo Rejection Reasons

1. Wrong Dimensions

Every exam has specific dimension requirements. For example, SSC CGL requires a 100×120 pixel photo, but if you upload a 200×230 pixel photo, it will be rejected. Always check the exact dimensions in the official notification.

2. File Size Too Large

Exam forms have a maximum file size limit. If the limit is 50 KB, uploading a 200 KB photo will result in rejection. You need to compress your photo to the exact size.

3. Wrong Format

Most exams only accept JPG or JPEG formats. If you upload a PNG, WebP, or GIF file, it will be rejected. Always save your photo as a JPG before uploading.

4. Blurry Photo

A low-resolution or blurry photo will be rejected. Your face must be clear and easily identifiable. Do not use a selfie or a photo taken with a low-quality camera.

5. Glasses or Cap

Wearing glasses, a cap, or any headgear in the photo will result in rejection. Your face must be clearly visible without any obstructions.

6. Old Photo

A photo that is 6 months or 1 year old will be rejected. Most exams require a photo taken within the last 3 months. If your appearance has changed, take a new photo.

7. Colored Background

A plain white or light-colored background is required. A red, blue, or dark background will be rejected. Make sure the background is clean and uniform.

8. Side Face or Turned Head

Your face must be looking straight at the camera. A side profile or turned head will be rejected.

9. Capital Letters in Signature

Your signature should be in running handwriting. Using CAPITAL LETTERS will result in rejection. Sign naturally on white paper with a black pen.

10. Blurry Signature

An unclear or faint signature will be rejected. Use black ink and sign clearly on white paper. Scan or photograph the signature at high resolution.

⚠️ Important: Always check all requirements before filling out an exam form. One small mistake can cause form rejection. Read the official notification carefully.
