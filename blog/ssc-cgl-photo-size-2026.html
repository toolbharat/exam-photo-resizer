SSC CGL Photo Size — Detailed Requirements

Photo (100 × 120 pixels, 20-50 KB)

  • Dimensions: 100 pixels width, 120 pixels height
  • File size: 20 KB se kam nahi, 50 KB se zyada nahi
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
  • Background: Plain white ya halka color
  • Face: Camera ki taraf seedha dekhte hue
  • Recent: 3 mahine ke andar ki photo
  • No glasses: Chashma pehna hua nahi
  • No cap/hat: Sir par kuch nahi

Signature (140 × 60 pixels, 10-20 KB)

  • Dimensions: 140 pixels width, 60 pixels height
  • File size: 10 KB se kam nahi, 20 KB se zyada nahi
  • Format: JPG ya JPEG
  • Ink: Black ink, white paper par
  • Style: Running handwriting — CAPITAL LETTERS nahi
  • Clear: Signature saaf aur readable

Common Mistakes — Inse Bachein

SSC CGL form reject hone ke sabse common karan:

  • Galat dimensions: Log 200×230 ya 320×240 px ka photo upload kar dete hain — reject
  • File size zyada: 100 KB ya 200 KB ka photo — reject
  • Signature mein CAPITAL letters: SSC rule hai running handwriting
  • Purani photo: 6 mahine se purani photo — reject
  • Chashma/hat: Photo mein chashma ya cap — reject
  • PNG format: SSC JPG maangta hai — PNG reject
  • Signature blur: Dhundhla signature — reject
⚠️ Important: SSC CGL photo aur signature form fill karne se pehle zaroor check karo. Ek chhoti galti form rejection ka karan ban sakti hai.

Nahi, 50 KB maximum limit hai. Zyada file size reject ho jati hai.
