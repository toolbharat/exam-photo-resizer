CGPA FAQ

CBSE ka CGPA to percentage formula kya hai?

CBSE: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Example: 8.5 CGPA = 80.75%.

Engineering ka formula kya hai?

Kai engineering universities: Percentage = (CGPA × 10) - 7.5. Apni university ki website se confirm karo.

CGPA 8.5 ka percentage kya hoga?

CBSE formula se: 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%. Engineering formula se: (8.5 × 10) - 7.5 = 77.5%.

Kya har university ka formula alag hota hai?

Haan, har university ka formula alag hota hai. Sahi formula apni university ki official website se check karo.
