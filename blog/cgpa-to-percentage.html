CGPA FAQ

What is the CBSE CGPA to percentage formula?

CBSE: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Example: 8.5 CGPA = 80.75%.

What is the engineering formula?

Many engineering universities use: Percentage = (CGPA × 10) - 7.5. Confirm with your university's website.

What is the percentage for 8.5 CGPA?

CBSE formula: 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%. Engineering formula: (8.5 × 10) - 7.5 = 77.5%.

Does every university have a different formula?

Yes, every university has a different formula. Check the correct formula on your university's official website.
