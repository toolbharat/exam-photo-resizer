Free Online Photo Merger - Combine Images & Signatures

PixSathi ka Photo Merger ek 100% free online tool hai jo aapko ek hi image mein do ya usse zyada photos aur signatures ko jodne (merge) ki suvidha deta hai. Chahe aapko exam form ke liye apni photo aur signature ek hi document mein chahiye, ya phir aap kisi project ke liye do alag-alag images ko ek sath jodna chahte hain, ye tool aapka kaam seconds mein kar deta hai. Sabse badi baat, is tool mein aapki images kisi server par upload nahi hoti. Sab kuch aapke browser mein securely process hota hai.

How to Merge Photos Online (Step-by-Step Guide)

  • Step 1: Upload Images - Tool ke upload box (+ Add Photo) par click karein aur apne computer ya mobile se un photos ko select karein jinhe aap merge karna chahte hain.
  • Step 2: Adjust & Arrange - Photos upload hone ke baad, aap direction (Horizontal, Vertical, Grid) select kar sakte hain aur unhe apni pasand ke hisaab se arrange kar sakte hain.
  • Step 3: Merge Photos - Jab aapki photos sahi position par aa jayein, toh "Join Images" ke button par click karein.
  • Step 4: Download - Aapki nayi combined image taiyar ho jayegi. Ab "Download Merged Photo" button dabakar us nayi image ko apne phone ya laptop mein save kar lein.
