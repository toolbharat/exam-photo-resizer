Make Signature White Background Online

Sarkari exam forms (SSC, IBPS, UPSC) mein aksar signature ka background safed (white) hona zaroori hota hai. Jab hum safed kaagaz par signature karke mobile se photo lete hain, toh shadow ki wajah se background grey ya dark aa jata hai. PixSathi ka Signature Background Remover ek click mein aapke signature ka background pure white aur ink ko pure black kar deta hai. Sab kuch aapke browser mein hota hai, aapka signature kisi server par upload nahi hota.

How to Clean Signature Background (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Apne signature ki photo upload karein.
  • Step 2: "Adjust Background Whiteness" slider ko aage badhayein (right side).
  • Step 3: Jab background ekdum white aur signature dark dikhe, toh "Download" button dabayein.
