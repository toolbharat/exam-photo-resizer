What is Pomodoro?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method. You study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After 4 sessions, take a longer break.

How to Use

  1. Click "Start" to begin a 25-minute study session
  2. Focus on your work
  3. When the timer ends, take a 5-minute break
  4. Repeat
