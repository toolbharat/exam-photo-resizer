Frequently Asked Questions

How to convert cm to feet?

To convert cm to feet, divide the number of centimeters by 30.48. For example, 170 cm ÷ 30.48 = 5.58 feet.

How to convert feet to cm?

To convert feet to cm, multiply the number of feet by 30.48. For example, 5.5 ft × 30.48 = 167.64 cm.
