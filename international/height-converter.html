Height Converter FAQ

How do I convert cm to feet?

Formula: feet = cm / 30.48. Example: 170 cm = 5.58 ft.

How do I convert feet to cm?

Formula: cm = feet × 30.48. Example: 5.5 ft = 167.64 cm.

How do I convert inches to cm?

Formula: cm = inches × 2.54. Example: 70 in = 177.8 cm.
