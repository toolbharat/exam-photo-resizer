What is a Unit Conversion Table?

A unit conversion table is a handy reference chart that lists the equivalent values of different measurement units (like cm to inches, or kg to lbs). While physical tables are useful, using an online unit converter calculator like ours is much faster and eliminates the risk of human error when doing the math manually.
