Free Final Grade Calculator

PixSathi's Grade Calculator is a 100% free online tool that helps students answer the question: "What grade do I need on my final exam?" Whether you are in high school or college, our tool calculates exactly what score you need on your final to achieve your target overall grade. It is the perfect free alternative to Calculator.net and RapidTables.

How to Use the Final Grade Calculator

  1. Enter your Current Grade (your grade before the final exam).
  2. Enter the Weight of Final Exam (e.g., if the final is worth 30% of your grade, enter 30).
  3. Enter the Grade You Want (your target final grade).
  4. Click "Calculate Needed Grade" to see the result instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what grade I need on my final?

To find out, you need your current grade, the weight of the final exam, and your desired grade. Our tool uses the formula: (Desired Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Weight))) ÷ Weight.

What if the calculator says I need more than 100%?

If the result is above 100%, it means achieving your desired grade is mathematically impossible unless there is extra credit available.
