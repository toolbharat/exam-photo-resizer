Free Word Counter & Character Counter

PixSathi's Word Counter is a 100% free online tool that helps you count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs instantly. Whether you are a student writing an essay, a blogger writing an article, or a professional writing a report, our tool makes it incredibly easy to keep track of your text length. If you are looking for a free alternative to WordCounter.net or Grammarly, you are at the right place.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. The tool will instantly count your words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs.
  3. No signup, no limits, and your text never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to count characters in your text?

Just paste your text into the box above. Our tool automatically counts both characters (with and without spaces) and words in real-time.

Is this a free alternative to WordCounter.net or Grammarly?

Yes, PixSathi is completely free. We don't require a signup, and there is no character limit. Your data is processed locally in your browser.

Is my text safe?

Absolutely. All counting happens instantly in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server.
