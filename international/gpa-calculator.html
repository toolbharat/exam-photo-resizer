Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate GPA?

To calculate GPA, multiply the grade point for each class by the credit hours, add them all up, and divide by the total number of credit hours.

What is a cumulative GPA?

Cumulative GPA (CGPA) is the average of all your grade points across all semesters or terms you have completed so far.

What is a weighted GPA?

A weighted GPA takes into account the difficulty of your courses. For example, an 'A' in an AP or Honors class might be worth 5.0 instead of 4.0.

What is a good GPA?

A GPA of 3.0 or higher is generally considered good, while a GPA of 3.5+ is excellent for college admissions.
