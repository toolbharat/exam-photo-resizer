How to Calculate Fuel Cost for a Trip

Calculating your trip fuel cost is easy. Here is the simple formula our tool uses:

  • Fuel Needed: Distance / Fuel Efficiency (km/L)
  • Total Cost: Fuel Needed × Fuel Price (per L)

For example, if you are traveling 100 km, your car gives 15 km/L, and fuel costs $1 per L, you will need 6.67 liters of fuel, costing a total of $6.67.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate gas cost for a trip?

Divide the total distance of your trip by your car's fuel efficiency (mpg or km/L) to find out how much fuel you need. Then multiply that by the current fuel price.

What is the formula for petrol cost?

The formula is: (Distance / Mileage) × Petrol Price. You can also just enter your numbers into the calculator above for instant results.

Is this a free alternative to GasBuddy?

Yes, PixSathi is completely free. While GasBuddy focuses on finding the cheapest gas prices near you, our tool focuses purely on calculating the total cost of your trip.
