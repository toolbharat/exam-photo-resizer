How to Calculate Tip Percentage

Calculating the tip percentage is straightforward. Here is the formula we use:

  • Tip Amount: Bill Amount × (Tip % / 100)
  • Total Bill (including tip): Bill Amount + Tip Amount
  • Amount per person: Total Bill / Number of People

For example, if your bill is $50 and you want to leave a 15% tip, the tip amount is $7.50, making your total bill $57.50. If you split it among 2 people, each person pays $28.75.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 15% tip?

To calculate a 15% tip, multiply your bill amount by 0.15. For a $50 bill, 15% is $7.50, making the total $57.50. You can also just use the calculator above.

How to calculate total amount with tip?

Enter your bill amount and desired tip percentage into the calculator. The tool will instantly show you the total amount including tip, as well as how much it is per person if you split the bill.

How do I split the bill evenly?

Simply enter the number of people in the "Split Between" field. The calculator will automatically divide the total bill (including tip) by the number of people and show you the amount each person owes.
