Convert Presentation to PDF Online Free

PixSathi ka Presentation to PDF tool aapko aapke PPT slides ko ek single PDF document me convert karne ki suvidha deta hai. Kyunki direct PPTX parsing browser me possible nahi hai, hum ye tarika use karte hain jisme aap apne slides ko images me save karke upload karte hain. Ye 100% free, fast aur private hai.

How to Convert PPT to PDF (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Apni PPT file open karein aur "Save as Images" (JPG/PNG) karein.
  • Step 2: Upar diye gaye box me apni saari slide images select karein.
  • Step 3: "Convert to PDF" button dabayein.
  • Step 4: Aapki saari slides ek PDF file me convert ho jayengi.
