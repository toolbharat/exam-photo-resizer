                                                  Things to Consider When Building a Resume

                                                  1. Resume Layout & Format

                                                  Consider the hiring manager's perspective and the qualities they might find helpful and easy to scan. Focus on the necessary details first. Create sections to list your professional skills, certifications, awards, memberships, education, and accomplishments in an organized and concise format.

                                                  2. What NOT to Include in Your Resume

                                                  • Do not include hobbies or objectives: Focus only on the necessary information relevant to the job.
                                                  • No photo: You don’t need to include a photo unless it’s necessary for specific industries.
                                                  • No emojis: Avoid using exclamation points, smiley faces, or emojis in your resume.
                                                  • Professional Email: Don’t use nicknames or catchphrases in your email address.
                                                  • Check formatting: Double-check the bullet points, periods, capitalization, spelling, and overall structure.
