Free Online Exam Marks Calculator (With Negative Marking)

PixSathi ka Exam Marks Calculator ek 100% free tool hai jo sarkari exam (jaise SSC CGL, IBPS PO, SBI Clerk, RRB NTPC, UPSC) ke students ke liye banaya gaya hai. Exam ke baad sabse bada sawaal yahi hota hai: "Mera score kitna banega?" Is tool mein aap apne Sahi (Correct) aur Galat (Wrong) answers daal kar apna exact score aur percentage seconds mein nikaal sakte hain. Isme aap alag-alag exams ki negative marking (0.25, 0.33, 0.50) bhi select kar sakte hain.

How to Calculate Your Exam Score (Step-by-Step)

  • Step 1: Apne exam ke total questions enter karein.
  • Step 2: Apne Sahi (Correct) answers ki sankhya daalein.
  • Step 3: Apne Galat (Wrong) answers ki sankhya daalein.
  • Step 4: Exam ki negative marking select karein (jaise SSC ke liye 0.25).
  • Step 5: "Calculate My Score" par click karein aur apna result dekhein.

Negative Marking Kya Hai?

Bahut se sarkari exams mein galat jawab dene par marks kate jaate hain, isko negative marking kehte hain. Alag-alag exams ki negative marking alag hoti hai. Jaise SSC exams mein har galat answer par 0.25 marks katte hain, jabki IBPS PO mein 0.33 marks katte hain. Is tool se aap apni exact negative marking nikaal kar apna final score jaan sakte hain bina kisi galti ke.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this tool accurate for all exams?

Yes, the formula used here is the standard one. Just select the correct negative marking for your exam (0.25, 0.33, or 0.50) and the score will be 100% accurate.

What is the formula for calculating marks?

Total Score = (Correct Answers × Marks per correct) - (Wrong Answers × Negative Marking).

Does it save my data?

No, this tool runs completely in your browser. We do not save or collect your answers or scores.
