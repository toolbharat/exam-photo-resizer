Check Exam Photo Size & Format

Sarkari exam forms mein photo aur signature reject hone ka sabse bada karan galat file size (KB) ya dimensions (pixels) hota hai. PixSathi ka Exam Photo Checker aapki photo aur signature dono ko instantly check karta hai. Agar aapka document sahi hai, toh ye green signal deta hai, aur agar galat hai, toh batata hai ki kya change karna hai.

Note: Exam rules can sometimes change. While we try our best to keep this updated, please always cross-check the exact photo size and dimensions from the official exam notification before submitting your form.
