RRB NTPC Photo Size 2026: Exact Requirements

Are you filling out the RRB NTPC (Railway Non-Technical Popular Categories) exam form? One of the most common reasons applications get rejected is uploading a photo or signature that doesn't meet the official specifications. The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is very strict about dimensions, file size, and format.

In this guide, we will give you the exact RRB NTPC photo size and signature requirements for 2026, and show you how to resize your images for free using our online tool.

⚡ Quick Answer: RRB NTPC photo = 100×120 pixels (15-40 KB), signature = 140×60 pixels (10-20 KB).

Photo Requirements — Detailed

  • Dimensions: 100 pixels wide and 120 pixels high.
  • File Size: Between 15 KB and 40 KB. A file outside this range will be rejected.
  • Format: Only JPG or JPEG formats are accepted.
  • Background: Plain white or light-colored background.
  • Face: Looking straight at the camera. No glasses, caps, or headgear.
  • Recency: The photo should be recent, taken within the last 3 months.
  • Quality: The photo must be clear and not blurry.

Signature Requirements — Detailed

  • Dimensions: 140 pixels wide and 60 pixels high.
  • File Size: Between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • Ink: Black ink on white paper.
  • Style: Running handwriting — not in CAPITAL LETTERS.
  • Clarity: The signature should be clear and readable.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong dimensions: Uploading a 200×230 px photo instead of 100×120 px will be rejected.
  • File size too large: A photo larger than 40 KB will be rejected.
  • CAPITAL letters in signature: RRB requires running handwriting.
  • Old photo: A photo older than 3 months may be rejected.
  • Glasses or cap: The face must be clearly visible without any obstruction.
  • PNG format: Only JPG/JPEG is accepted. Do not use PNG.
