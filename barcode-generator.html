Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Which barcode format should I use?

For retail products in India, use EAN-13. For internal inventory or alphanumeric codes, use Code 128.

Are these barcodes scannable?

Yes, these are standard barcodes and can be scanned by any barcode scanner.
