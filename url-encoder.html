What is URL Encoding?

URLs me sirf certain characters allowed hote hain. Agar aap koi space ya special character use karte hain, toh browser use galat samajh sakta hai. URL Encoding wo process hai jisme aise characters ko %20 (space ke liye) ya %26 (& ke liye) me convert kiya jata hai. Decoding iska ulta hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between encodeURI and encodeURIComponent?

encodeURI poori URL ko encode karta hai (jaise https:// ko chhod deta hai), jabki encodeURIComponent URL ke parameters ko encode karta hai. Ye tool query parameters ke liye best hai.
