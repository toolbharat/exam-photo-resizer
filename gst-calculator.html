How to Calculate GST (Formula)

  • Add GST (Exclusive): Formula = Amount + (Amount × GST Rate / 100)
  • Remove GST (Reverse/Inclusive): Formula = Total Amount - [Total Amount × (100 / (100 + GST Rate))]
  • CGST & SGST: Total GST / 2 (For Intra-state transactions)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Reverse GST Calculator?

Reverse GST calculation is used when the price of a product already includes GST, and you need to find out the original base price and the tax amount included in it.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

CGST and SGST are collected on sales within the same state (split equally). IGST is collected on sales between two different states.
