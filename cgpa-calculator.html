Common CGPA to Percentage Formulas

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my CGPA?

To calculate CGPA, add the grade points for all subjects and divide by the total number of subjects. You can then use this tool to convert it to a percentage.

What is the CBSE formula for CGPA to percentage?

The CBSE formula is: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. For example, if your CGPA is 8.5, your percentage is 80.75%.

What is a good CGPA?

A CGPA of 8.0 or above (on a 10-point scale) is considered very good and opens up opportunities for higher education and jobs.
