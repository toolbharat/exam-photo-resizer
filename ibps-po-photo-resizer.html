IBPS PO Photo Requirements

IBPS PO form bharne se pehle in requirements ko dhyan se padhein:

  • Photo Size: Between 20KB to 50KB.
  • Dimensions: 200x230 pixels (Width x Height).
  • Format: JPEG/JPG only.
  • Background: Light colored or white.
