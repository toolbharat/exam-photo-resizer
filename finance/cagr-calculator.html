What is CAGR?

CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is the average annual growth rate of an investment over a specific period. It smooths out the returns and gives a single number that represents how much your investment grew each year.

Formula

CAGR = [(Final Value / Initial Value)^(1/n) − 1] × 100

  • n = Number of years

Why CAGR is Useful

  • Compare different investments over the same period
  • Measure mutual fund and stock performance
  • Track business revenue growth
  • Plan long-term financial goals

CAGR vs Absolute Return

  • Absolute Return: Total return (e.g., 100% in 5 years)
  • CAGR: Average annual return (e.g., 14.87% per year)
  • CAGR gives a better picture of performance over time

Important Note

CAGR assumes steady growth, but in reality, investments fluctuate. It is a useful metric for comparison, but it does not show the volatility of returns.
