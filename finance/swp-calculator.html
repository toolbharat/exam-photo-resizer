What is SWP?

SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) is a facility where you withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund investment at regular intervals. It is commonly used by retirees to generate a regular income from their corpus.

How SWP Works

  • You invest a lump sum in a mutual fund scheme
  • You set a fixed withdrawal amount (monthly, quarterly, etc.)
  • The remaining corpus continues to earn returns
  • The corpus depletes over time depending on withdrawal vs returns

Why SWP is Useful

  • Regular income: Like a pension from your investments
  • Tax efficient: Only the capital gains portion is taxed
  • Flexible: You can change withdrawal amount anytime
  • Better than FD: Potentially higher returns than fixed deposits

Important Note

SWP returns are market-linked and not guaranteed. If the fund performs poorly, your corpus may deplete faster than expected. Consider a conservative withdrawal rate (4-6% annually) for long-term sustainability.
