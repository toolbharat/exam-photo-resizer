What is RD?

RD (Recurring Deposit) is a savings scheme offered by banks and post offices where you deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed tenure. It earns interest at a rate similar to fixed deposits and is ideal for regular savers.

Key Features

  • Monthly deposit: Fixed amount every month
  • Tenure: 6 months to 10 years
  • Interest rate: Similar to FD rates (7-8% typically)
  • Compounding: Quarterly (most banks)
  • Minimum deposit: ₹100 per month (varies by bank)

RD vs FD

  • FD: One-time lump sum deposit
  • RD: Monthly deposits over the tenure
  • Both: Same interest rates, same tax treatment

Tax on RD Interest

  • Interest is taxable as per your income tax slab
  • TDS (10%) applies if annual interest exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens)
  • No TDS if you submit Form 15G/15H

Important Note

RD interest rates vary by bank and tenure. Senior citizens usually get 0.5% higher rates. This calculator uses quarterly compounding, which is standard for most banks.
