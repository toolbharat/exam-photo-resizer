What is FOIR?

FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the percentage of your monthly income that lenders allow to be used for EMI payments. A lower FOIR means you can safely take a larger loan.

  • Home Loan: 50% of income
  • Car Loan: 40% of income
  • Personal Loan: 35% of income
