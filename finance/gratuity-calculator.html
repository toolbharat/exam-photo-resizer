What is Gratuity?

Gratuity is a lump sum payment made by an employer to an employee as a token of appreciation for their service. It is governed by the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

Eligibility

  • Employee must have completed 5 years of continuous service
  • Payable on retirement, resignation, superannuation, or death
  • Exception: 5-year rule waived in case of death or disability

Formula

Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Salary × Years of Service) / 26

  • 15 days' salary for each completed year
  • 26 = number of working days in a month
  • Last drawn salary = Basic + Dearness Allowance

Tax Exemption

  • Gratuity up to ₹20 lakh is tax-free for eligible employees
  • Amount above ₹20 lakh is taxable as per income tax rules
  • Applies to employees covered under the Gratuity Act

Important Note

If your service is more than 6 months in the last year, it is rounded up to the next full year. If less than 6 months, it is ignored.
