What is PPF?

PPF (Public Provident Fund) is a long-term savings scheme backed by the Government of India. It offers guaranteed returns and is one of the most popular tax-saving instruments under Section 80C.

Key Features

  • Tenure: 15 years (extendable in blocks of 5 years)
  • Minimum investment: ₹500 per year
  • Maximum investment: ₹1,50,000 per year
  • Interest rate: Government-notified, revised quarterly (currently ~7.1%)
  • Tax status: EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)

Tax Benefits

  • Investment: Deductible under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh)
  • Interest: Fully tax-free
  • Maturity: Fully tax-free
  • Note: 80C deduction is available only under the Old Tax Regime

PPF Interest Calculation

PPF interest is calculated on the minimum balance between the 5th and last day of each month. Interest is credited annually on 31st March.

Important Note

PPF interest rates are subject to change every quarter. The calculator uses the rate you enter. Check the current rate from the National Savings Institute before investing.
