What is PPF and Why is it a Great Investment?

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is one of the most popular long-term savings schemes in India. Backed by the Government of India, it offers a safe and secure way to build wealth over time. Whether you are saving for your retirement, your child's education, or simply want to park your money in a risk-free instrument, PPF is an excellent choice. Our PPF Calculator helps you estimate the maturity amount and the total interest earned over your investment period.

In this guide, we will explore how PPF works, its tax benefits, and why it should be a part of your financial portfolio.

How Does the PPF Work?

PPF is a 15-year investment scheme. You can open an account at any bank or post office. Every year, you must deposit a minimum of ₹500 and a maximum of ₹1,50,000. The interest rate is set by the government and revised quarterly (currently around 7.1%). The interest is compounded annually, and you receive the entire maturity amount at the end of 15 years. You can also extend the tenure in blocks of 5 years after maturity.

Tax Benefits of PPF: The EEE Advantage

PPF falls under the Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) category, making it one of the most tax-efficient investments available:

  • Investment: Your annual contribution up to ₹1.5 lakh is deductible under Section 80C (available only under the Old Tax Regime).
  • Interest: The interest earned every year is completely tax-free.
  • Maturity: The maturity amount you receive is also completely tax-free.

This triple tax advantage means you don't pay a single rupee in taxes on your PPF returns.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can I withdraw money before 15 years?
A: Partial withdrawals are allowed after 5 years for specific purposes like education or medical treatment. However, the account matures after 15 years.

Q: What happens if I don't deposit money in a year?
A: If you fail to deposit the minimum ₹500 in a year, your account becomes inactive. You can revive it by paying a penalty fee and the minimum deposit.

Q: Is PPF better than FD?
A: PPF offers higher post-tax returns compared to FD because the interest is completely tax-free. However, FD is more flexible with shorter tenures.

Q: Can I open a PPF account for my child?
A: Yes, you can open a PPF account on behalf of a minor child. The account matures when the child turns 18.
