EMI Calculator: Plan Your Loan Repayment

Whether it's a home loan, car loan, or personal loan, our EMI Calculator helps you understand your monthly outgo. Enter the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to see your monthly EMI, total interest payable, and total payment. You can also see a year-wise breakdown of principal and interest to understand how your loan works.

SIP Calculator: Grow Your Wealth with Mutual Funds

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is one of the best ways to build wealth over time. Our SIP Calculator shows you how your monthly investments can grow with the power of compounding. Enter your monthly amount, expected return rate, and investment period to see your estimated maturity value.

Income Tax Calculator: Save More Tax

Tax planning is crucial for every salaried individual. Our Income Tax Calculator compares the Old and New Tax Regime, so you can choose the one that saves you more money. It accounts for all major deductions like 80C, 80D, and home loan interest.

FD, RD, and PPF Calculators: Safe Investments

For risk-averse investors, fixed-income instruments like Fixed Deposits (FD), Recurring Deposits (RD), and Public Provident Fund (PPF) are excellent choices. Our calculators show you exactly how much you will earn over time, including the tax implications.

GST Calculator: For Businesses and Freelancers

If you run a business or work as a freelancer, calculating GST is a daily task. Our GST Calculator instantly computes the net price, GST amount, and gross price for any of the standard GST slabs (5%, 12%, 18%, 28%).

Salary Calculator: Know Your In-Hand Salary

Confused between CTC and take-home salary? Our Salary Calculator breaks down your salary components (basic, HRA, special allowance, PF, professional tax) to show you exactly how much money will be credited to your bank account each month.

Loan Eligibility Calculator: Check How Much You Can Borrow

Before applying for a loan, it's important to know how much you are eligible for. Our Loan Eligibility Calculator uses your monthly income and existing EMIs to estimate the maximum loan amount banks may offer you.
